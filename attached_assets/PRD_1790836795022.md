# AEGIS — Project Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Summary
**AEGIS** is a privacy-conscious, accessible support platform for adults and older adults who may live alone or whose children/family live in another city. It connects the user with family, trusted local support, essential services, routine assistance, and emergency escalation through a simple phone/tablet experience.

**Core promise:** provide a protective support layer without taking away the user's independence.

## 2. Problem
Physical distance between older adults and their family can make everyday needs harder to coordinate. Problems include missed check-ins, difficulty arranging groceries/medicines/transport, fragmented communication, delayed assistance, and uncertainty during emergencies.

AEGIS addresses this as a **coordination and access problem**, not as a replacement for family, doctors, or emergency services.

## 3. Target Users
### Primary
- Adults/older adults, approximately 40–70+, who benefit from simple digital assistance.
- People living alone or with limited day-to-day family support.

### Secondary
- Adult children, relatives, or designated caregivers living in another city.
- Trusted neighbours/community contacts.
- Pharmacies, grocery/meal services, transport and home-support providers.
- Professional care providers where the workflow requires them.

## 4. Product Goals
1. Make common support requests understandable and easy to complete.
2. Keep family communication predictable without requiring constant calls.
3. Create a configurable, human-led emergency escalation path.
4. Reduce fragmented coordination between family and local support.
5. Minimise the amount of personal data collected and shared.
6. Meet a strong accessibility baseline for older and low-vision users.
7. Avoid misleading health, safety, AI, or service claims.

## 5. Non-Goals
- A diagnostic medical device.
- Independent medical diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
- Replacement for emergency services.
- Continuous surveillance of users.
- Selling personal or health data.
- Collecting health information merely because the product can.

## 6. MVP Features
### A. User Home
- Today's tasks and reminders.
- Family contact shortcuts.
- Help/service shortcuts.
- Clearly visible emergency/help action.
- Minimal information density.

### B. Family
- Approved family contacts.
- Call/message shortcuts.
- Configurable communication routines.
- Agreed status/check-in visibility.

### C. Help & Services
- Grocery/meal assistance.
- Medicine/prescription pickup where legally and operationally appropriate.
- Transport.
- Home assistance.
- Trusted local service directory.
- Request status and acknowledgement.

### D. Daily Check-In
- Scheduled check-in.
- One-tap completion.
- Optional voice interaction.
- Missed-check-in escalation according to user-configured rules.

### E. Emergency Escalation
- User-configured escalation chain.
- Alert acknowledgement tracking.
- Human-first escalation.
- Clear disclaimer that AEGIS does not replace emergency services.

### F. Profile & Permissions
- User profile.
- Trusted contacts.
- Data-sharing permissions.
- Notification preferences.
- Language/accessibility preferences.
- Delete/export/request mechanisms as applicable.

### G. Consent & Legal
The production website/app must include:
- Privacy Policy.
- Terms & Conditions.
- Cookie Policy.
- Cookie consent/preferences where non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies require consent.
- Refund/cancellation policy if paid services are offered.
- Form-specific consent where consent is the legal basis or otherwise appropriate.
- Clear data-use notices.
- Business/operator identity and contact details.
- Grievance/support contact.

## 7. Data Minimisation
Collect only information required for a stated product function.

Potentially necessary:
- Name and contact details.
- Trusted contact details.
- Service request information.
- Appointment/task details when required.
- Account/security data.

Potentially sensitive:
- Health information.
- Precise location.
- Emergency information.
- Medication information.

Sensitive data must **not** be collected by default. Each field must have a documented purpose, retention period, access scope, and lawful processing basis.

## 8. Analytics & Tracking
Default position: **privacy-preserving and minimal analytics**.

Rules:
- No advertising trackers in MVP.
- No session replay by default.
- No collection of health data through analytics.
- No analytics identifiers in sensitive URLs or payloads.
- Load non-essential analytics only when the required consent conditions are satisfied.
- Document every third-party analytics/SDK dependency.

## 9. Third-Party Content
Before using an embed or SDK, document:
- Provider.
- Purpose.
- Data transmitted.
- Cookies/storage used.
- Geographic processing location where relevant.
- Contract/DPA status where applicable.
- Whether consent is required.
- Fallback if the provider is unavailable.

Avoid third-party embeds when a self-hosted or simpler alternative exists.

## 10. Accessibility Requirements
Target **WCAG 2.2 AA** as the engineering baseline.

Required:
- Keyboard-accessible functionality.
- Visible and sufficiently contrasting focus indicators.
- Descriptive labels.
- Text alternatives/alt text for meaningful images.
- Minimum text contrast requirements.
- Non-text UI contrast.
- Large, well-spaced touch targets.
- No colour-only communication.
- Predictable navigation.
- Clear validation/error messages.
- Screen-reader-friendly semantic HTML.
- Responsive phone/tablet layouts.
- Text scaling without breaking the interface.

## 11. UX Requirements
- Calm, familiar healthcare/service UI.
- Large typography.
- Short sentences.
- Clear labelled icons.
- One primary action per card where possible.
- Avoid hidden gestures.
- Avoid dense dashboards.
- Avoid unnecessary animations.
- Clear confirmation after consequential actions.
- Voice interaction is an enhancement, not the only method.

## 12. Content & Trust Requirements
Must remove or prevent:
- Fake reviews/testimonials.
- Unsupported statistics.
- Unverified medical/safety claims.
- "Guaranteed" outcomes.
- Fabricated partner/client logos.
- Artificial urgency or dark patterns.
- Stock imagery presented as real users.
- Copyright-infringing images/assets.

All claims should be traceable to evidence or clearly presented as product intent.

## 13. Paid Services
If AEGIS becomes a paid product:
- Clearly disclose price, billing interval, taxes/fees where applicable, cancellation terms and refund eligibility.
- Implement a refund/cancellation policy appropriate to the actual product and jurisdiction.
- Do not promise a refund rule until the commercial model and applicable law are confirmed.

## 14. Business Details
Production footer and legal pages should identify:
- Legal business/entity name.
- Registered/business address where required.
- Support email.
- Privacy contact/DPO contact where required.
- Grievance/contact mechanism where applicable.
- Applicable company registration details if legally required.

## 15. Success Metrics
- Check-in completion rate.
- Help-request acknowledgement time.
- Request resolution rate.
- Emergency-alert acknowledgement time.
- Family communication completion rate.
- Task completion without unnecessary family intervention.
- Accessibility usability findings.
- Consent/privacy incidents: target zero.

## 16. Acceptance Criteria
A release is not considered complete if:
- Required legal pages are missing.
- Required consent controls are missing.
- Unsupported claims remain.
- Accessibility checks fail materially.
- Sensitive data is collected without a documented purpose and legal basis.
- Third-party tracking is undocumented.
- Copyright/licensing status of production assets is unknown.
- Business/operator details are missing.

## 17. Jurisdiction / Compliance Baseline
The initial deployment assumption is **India**, with the architecture designed so additional jurisdictions can be supported.

The compliance review must account for:
- India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, subject to their applicable commencement dates.
- India consumer/e-commerce requirements if AEGIS sells goods/services online.
- GDPR/ePrivacy obligations if services are offered to people in the EU/EEA.
- U.S. state privacy laws when applicable to the users/business model.

Legal text must be reviewed for the actual entity, processing model, user locations, and commercial model before production launch.

- Whether payments/subscriptions are included in MVP.

- Exact analytics provider, if any.
